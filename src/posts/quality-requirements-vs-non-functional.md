Often the terms Quality Requirements (QRs) and Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs) are used interchangeably, something I am knowlingly guitly of as a teacher.
Generally the concepts are the same, they are requirements for a software product that do not relate to the functionality the client or end-user explicitly expects.
'Explicit' is important here, all clients or end-users have implicit expectations about functionality as well - often these are things that other systems already do that we pay little attention to and take for granted.
